Studio

A studio built around one architect's attention.

“Architecture is not just about creating spaces. It is about shaping experiences, emotions and lifestyles.”

AYG Design Studio works across residential architecture, interiors, space planning and construction — each project carried from first sketch to final handover by a single, principal-led team. Based in Barnala, Punjab, the studio takes on a deliberately small number of projects at a time, so that every drawing, material choice and site visit gets the attention it deserves.
